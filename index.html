Let Go — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

Let Go is a meditation app that helps you track your body's response during mindfulness sessions. Your privacy is fundamental to how the app is built.

Data We Collect

Let Go requests access to the following data solely to provide its core features:

  • Health data — heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), blood oxygen (SpO₂), and respiratory rate, read from Apple HealthKit via Apple Watch during active sessions.
  • Microphone — used in real time to detect your breathing rate and depth. Audio is processed on-device and never recorded or stored.

How Your Data Is Used

All data collected by Let Go is used exclusively to display your session results and history within the app. Specifically:

  • Vital samples are saved locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.
  • Microphone input is analysed in real time only — no audio is ever saved.
  • Your health and session data never leaves your device.

Data Sharing

Let Go does not share, sell, transmit, or disclose your data to any third party — ever. There are no analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, and no external servers in this app.

Data Storage & Deletion

All session data is stored locally on your iPhone using SwiftData. You can delete all app data at any time by removing Let Go from your device. Deleting the app permanently removes all stored session history.

Apple HealthKit

Let Go reads health data from Apple HealthKit with your explicit permission. We do not write data to HealthKit. Health data accessed through HealthKit is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy in addition to this policy.

Children's Privacy

Let Go is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date.
